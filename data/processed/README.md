# Processed data

The CSV files in this directory are generated by `run_processed_data.py` from `results/tables/` as the final data-export step in `scripts/reproduce.py`. Do not edit them manually. Regenerate with the notebook or `python3 scripts/reproduce.py`.

- `AB_comparison.csv`: 18 per-run rows, six `summary_by_g_arch` rows, and four `paired_difference` rows (one per field and one pooled). `shot_sem` is per-run Pauli sampling SEM; pooled sample SD/SEM summarize initialization variation.
- `noisy_error_decomposition.csv`: per-run exact-vs-sampled ZZ and X energy components, their signed error contributions, closure residual, and component/combined shot SEM.
- `graph_descriptors.csv`: graph statistics calculated from the frozen processor JSON edge lists.
- `E4_ansatz_patterns.csv`, `E4_pattern_summary.csv`, and `E4_paired_differences.csv` are supplementary and copied only after the separate E4 runner has generated them. They are not part of the default experiment run.

Column definitions, units, aggregation, seeds, and provenance are in `docs/DATA_DICTIONARY.md`. Raw VQE parameters, optimizer history, and sampled counts remain in `results/raw/`.
