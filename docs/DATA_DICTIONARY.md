# Data dictionary and provenance

## Generated result tables

- `results/tables/exact_crosscheck.csv`: one row per field. `dense_ground_energy`, `sparse_ground_energy`, `qiskit_pauli_ground_energy`, and `free_fermion_ground_energy` are ground energies in units with J=1. The `sparse_ground_energy` export is rounded to 12 decimal places to remove irrelevant eigensolver low-bit variation across clean copies; exact validations run before this serialization rounding. Spectrum-difference columns are maximum absolute differences between full sorted spectra. Magnetizations and correlators are expectation values; the guide leaves the magnetization axis and normalization undefined, so `magnetization_x_sum`, `magnetization_x_per_site`, and `mz_squared_over_n2` are explicit project conventions.
- `results/tables/ideal_architecture_results.csv`: three logical and six compiled rows for each `(g, seed)`; nine initialization runs total. `optimizer_success` is the optimizer's termination flag. `meets_project_energy_tolerance` is a separate derived project criterion, not an organizer requirement. `approximation_or_error` is absolute ideal energy error `|E_VQE-E_0|`; signed error is `E_VQE-E_0`. `cx_count`, `depth`, routing SWAP evidence, final-circuit SWAP gates, pass traces, layout and semantic fidelity refer to the mapped circuit. Timing fields are excluded so recorded numerical artifacts do not encode machine/runtime variance.
- `results/tables/noisy_results.csv`: one row per `(g, initialization seed, architecture)`. Energies are in J units; `standard_error_z` and `standard_error_x` are per-group Pauli sampling SEMs and `standard_error` combines them in quadrature. Each energy uses 4096 Z shots and 4096 X shots, with independent simulator seeds. Noisy-state fidelity is unavailable from Pauli samples and is explicitly not reported.
- `results/tables/AB_comparison.csv`: `row_type=per_run` contains each of the 18 noisy observations; `summary_by_g_arch` contains mean, sample SD and SEM across three initialization seeds for each field/architecture; `paired_difference` is sampled A energy minus B energy matched on field and VQE seed. The `g=all` row pools nine matched pairs. Between-start SEM is not the same as the shot SEM stored on individual rows.
- `results/tables/noisy_error_decomposition.csv`: `exact_zz_energy` and `exact_x_energy` are exact Hamiltonian contributions; `sampled_zz_energy` and `sampled_x_energy` are estimated contributions. Their differences from exact give signed component error contributions. `closure_residual` checks that the components sum to total noisy signed error. Component and total shot SEMs are given separately.
- `results/tables/graph_descriptors.csv`: node/edge counts, degree sequence, connectivity/path descriptors, motif placements and exact edge-list JSON computed from `processors/processor_*.json`. These describe topology, not device calibration or performance.
- `results/supplementary/tables/E4_ansatz_patterns.csv`: 54 compiled/noisy rows across three ansatz patterns, three fields, three starts and two fixed architectures. Optimizer status, ideal energy error, compiled CX/depth/routing SWAPs, semantic preservation, noisy energy/error and shot SEM are per row. The path parameters are reused from Stage 1; ring/star are each optimized once per `(g, seed)` and mapped identically to A and B.
- `results/supplementary/tables/E4_pattern_summary.csv`: means, sample SD/SEM, start counts, and minimum semantic fidelity over nine starts per pattern/architecture. Between-start statistics are distinct from per-run shot SEM.
- `results/supplementary/tables/E4_paired_differences.csv`: sampled A-minus-B energy differences matched by `(g, seed)`, with field-specific and pooled rows for each pattern. The paired SEM is across the matched field/seed observations, not shot uncertainty.
- `results/supplementary/raw/E4_ansatz_pattern_runs.json`: stored optimizer initialization/final parameters, evidence, and measured count dictionaries. Simulator seeds are generated with `derive_simulator_seed`; the pattern is included in the variant token to avoid seed collisions.

## Seeds and runs

The canonical VQE initialization seeds are 42, 7, and 21 for each `g` value 0.5, 1.0, and 1.5. They are independent starts, not repetitions after an optimizer failure. A separate restart diagnostic uses saved parameters and is never substituted into the nine canonical rows. The transpiler seed is 42. Noisy simulator seeds are derived by `src.randomness.derive_simulator_seed` using field, VQE seed, architecture, basis, batch and noise variant; Z and X are independent.

## Source/provenance

The model parameters and open-boundary Hamiltonian are transcribed from the Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 Phase 1 Master Challenge Guide dated October 14, 2026, page 5. A raw JSON copy of those inputs is in `data/raw/hamiltonian_s1.json`. Processor A/B graph examples, allowed basis and synthetic noise settings are transcribed from page 2 and preserved in `processors/processor_A.json` and `processor_B.json`. The exact cross-checks are generated from source code, not typed into this dictionary. The supplied ZIP and `Problem_Statment_Details.pdf` were not present in the workspace; no ZIP-based provenance check is claimed.
